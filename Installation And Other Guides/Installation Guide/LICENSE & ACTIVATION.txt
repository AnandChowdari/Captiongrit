-------------------------------------------------------------
  ACTIVATING YOUR LICENSE
-------------------------------------------------------------

  When you first open Captiongrit, you'll see the 
  activation screen. Enter the following to unlock the plugin:

  1. EMAIL ADDRESS
     The email address you used when purchasing the plugin.

  2. LICENSE KEY
     The license key you received after purchase.
     Format: CAP-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX

  3. Click "Activate License"

  If activation is successful, the main Captiongrit
  panel will appear and you can start generating captions.



-------------------------------------------------------------
  WHERE TO FIND YOUR LICENSE KEY
-------------------------------------------------------------

  Your license key was sent to you via:
    • The purchase confirmation email from the marketplace
    • The download page after completing your purchase

  If you cannot find your license key, contact the seller
  on the marketplace where you purchased Captiongrit.



-------------------------------------------------------------
  DEVICE LIMITS
-------------------------------------------------------------

  Your license allows activation on a limited number of 
  devices (typically 2 devices per license).

  If you reach the device limit:
    • Deactivate the plugin on a device you no longer use
      (Settings → Deactivate License)
    • Then activate on your new device

  If you're locked out, contact the seller for assistance.



-------------------------------------------------------------
  DEACTIVATING YOUR LICENSE
-------------------------------------------------------------

  To deactivate Captiongrit on a device:

  1. Open Captiongrit in After Effects or Premiere Pro
  2. Go to the "Settings" tab
  3. Scroll down to "License Information"
  4. Click "Deactivate License"
  5. The plugin will return to the activation screen

  This frees up a device slot so you can activate on 
  another computer.
